FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A little help.

This is an academic e-commerce project. Orders are saved in a real database, but no payment or shipment is processed.
Enter your order reference and private tracking key on the Track Order page. Both appear after checkout. On the same browser, the key is filled automatically from your saved order.
Orders are saved in Supabase. A local copy of the reference and tracking key is kept in your browser for convenient access.
Choose Compare on a product card or product detail page. You can compare up to four products.
Choose the heart icon to save a product. Your wishlist stays in this browser.
The backend checks inventory before saving an order and rejects quantities that exceed the available demonstration stock.
