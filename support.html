QUICK ANSWERS

Common support questions

How do I track an order?

Open Track Order and enter the order reference created when your order was placed.

Can I save products for later?

Yes. Use the heart icon on any product card or product page to add it to your wishlist.

How does product comparison work?

Select the compare icon on up to four product cards, then open the Compare page to review specifications side by side.

What is the return window?

TechNest provides a 30-day return window for eligible items. See the FAQ and Terms pages for details.
