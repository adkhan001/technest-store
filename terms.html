A few things to know.

Academic demonstration

TechNest demonstrates catalog browsing, comparison, checkout and database-backed order tracking. Placing an order creates a database record; it does not create a purchase, charge or shipment.

Product details

Product images and starting descriptions are sourced from the DummyJSON demonstration catalog. The collection represents actual device models. Sample prices and inventory are not commercial offers or verified market availability. Manufacturer names belong to their respective owners.

Orders

The backend calculates totals from stored product prices and checks available demonstration inventory. Keep your tracking key private. Status changes occur only when the project operator updates an order.

No payment collection

Cash on delivery is shown as a checkout example. No money is collected, and delivery and returns are not performed.
