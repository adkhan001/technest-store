PRIVACY

Your information.

What stays on your device

Your shopping bag, wishlist, comparison list, recent products, saved delivery details and order tracking keys are stored in your browser. Clearing your browser storage removes these local records.

What is stored in the database

When you place an order, your contact details, delivery address, product selection and total are saved in Supabase. Contact messages and newsletter email addresses are also stored there.

Who can read it

The product catalog is public. Orders, messages and subscriber records are restricted to the backend. Order lookup requires both an order reference and its private tracking key and does not return delivery or contact details.

Payments and cookies

This academic store does not collect card details or process payments. It uses browser storage for store features and does not include advertising trackers.
