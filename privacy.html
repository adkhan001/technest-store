Privacy

TechNest uses browser storage to remember cart items, wishlist selections, comparison choices, profile details and locally created orders on the device you are using.

Contact form entries are saved locally in this front-end build unless you connect the form to your own server or form provider. Before publishing a production store, connect these workflows to your privacy policy, backend, data-retention process and consent controls.

You can clear locally stored TechNest data through your browser settings.
